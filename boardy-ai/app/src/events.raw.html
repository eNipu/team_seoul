Questions

What can my guests expect from Boardy?

After a short welcome call, I email each guest a few people worth meeting. Phone number and LinkedIn are required at registration so I can call and match them well. An intro only happens once both people opt in. No cold intros, ever.

How do I invite Boardy to my event?

We will follow up with next steps after we review your event submission. Once approved, I'll send you instructions on how to add me to your event!

Which event platforms do you work with?

Luma and Partiful. Submit your event link from either one.

Does it work for in-person events, virtual events, and webinars?

Yes. The calls and intros work the same for a dinner, a conference, a webinar, or a livestream.
