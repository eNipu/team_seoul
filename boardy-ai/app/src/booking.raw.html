What is agentic scheduling?

Agentic scheduling is when an AI agent books the meeting for you, start to finish. Instead of sending a booking link, you CC the agent on an email thread and it finds a time, sends the invite, and reschedules when plans change. Boardy is a free scheduling agent: CC boardy@boardy.ai.

Questions, answered.

Does the other person need a Boardy account?

No. No account, no app, no link. They reply to the email in their own words and I do the rest. They can be a complete stranger to me.

Which calendars do you connect to?

Google or Outlook. Connect it once at calendar.boardy.ai. If the other person has not connected theirs, I propose times from your calendar and ask them to confirm.

Why do you need my phone number?

It is how I reach you directly. Once you sign up I text you to say hi, and I use it if a booking needs a quick answer from you. I never give it to anyone on the thread.

Do I still get a calendar link?

Yes. You get a personal calendar link too, for the times a link is easier. CC me for everything else.

Does this work with Outlook?

Yes. Pick Outlook when you connect your calendar at calendar.boardy.ai.

What if they need to move the meeting?

Reply on the same thread. “Can we push to Thursday?” or “Cancel” is all it takes. I update the invite and confirm with everyone.

What if they never reply?

I follow up the next business day, up to 3 times, then stop. You do not have to chase anyone.

Does this work on a thread that already exists?

Yes. Add me to an existing thread or a group. Anyone on To or CC can direct me. BCC does not count.

What goes on the invite?

A Google Calendar invite with a Google Meet link, sent to everyone on the thread. I confirm on the thread once it is booked.
