Turn past connections into new business.

You’ve already met your next client. Heldly keeps up with the people you know and tells you, in the ChatGPT or Claude chat you already use, when one of them needs what you do.

How a past connection becomes a meeting

  1. You tell your AI what you’re working on

    Heldly drafts short updates like “Looking for a design partner in Berlin.” You approve each one before anyone sees it.

  2. Your network stays current

    The people you’re connected to do the same. Their updates stay fresh without anyone posting or checking a feed.

  3. Heldly finds the opening

    Ask who could use your help, or let Heldly tell you. It shows the person, what changed, and how long since you last spoke.

  4. It books the meeting

    Pick a time. When the other person says yes, it lands on both calendars.

Most good connections stop after one meeting

  1. That evening

    A great conversation. You say “let’s stay in touch” and you mean it.

  2. The next day

    You swap LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

  3. A month later

    They start a company. You start looking for clients. Neither of you knows.

  4. A year later

    You’re still looking. They hired someone else.

Nobody stopped caring. Nothing told either of you that the other could help. Heldly is that reminder, with the context to act on it.

No new app. Ask in the chat you already have open.

Heldly runs inside ChatGPT and Claude. You add it once, then talk to it like anything else.

  • Who do I know working on AI tools in Berlin?
  • Anyone in my network hiring a fractional CFO?
  • Who haven’t I talked to in a year that I should?
  • Add to Heldly: I’m looking for two pilot customers in retail.
  • Set up 30 minutes with Mina next week.
  • What’s new with the people I met at the Seoul summit?

Who sees what

Shared, after you approve it

  • Short updates you approved, such as “Looking for a design partner in Berlin.”
  • Only with people you’re mutually connected to. A one-sided follow sees nothing.
  • You can delete an update or remove a connection at any time, and it disappears from their results.

Never shared

  • Your conversations with ChatGPT or Claude. Heldly receives only what you confirm.
  • Drafts you turn down.
  • Private notes about your contacts.
  • There is no public profile and no searchable directory.

Where it stands

Heldly is an early build, made for small professional communities first: accelerators, founder groups and cohorts where people already know each other.
