A plugin for ChatGPT

Turn past connections into new business.

Heldly remembers what you choose to tell it, groups it into your interests, and shows you how to keep each connection alive and where it could lead.

See it inside ChatGPT What works today

From what you say to who to call

  1. It remembers what you approve

    Talk to ChatGPT as usual. When something matters, like a new project, a city or a hobby, Heldly suggests a short memory. You decide what’s kept.

  2. Memories become interests

    What you’ve saved groups itself into the things you care about: climbing, AI tools, Berlin. That’s your map.

  3. It suggests the next move

    For each person, Heldly shows what you share, one way to keep the connection alive, and how it could help either of you.

Your network, inside ChatGPT

Heldly shows its own cards right in the chat. There’s no other app to open.

You decide what’s kept

  • It can’t read your chats

    Heldly only receives what ChatGPT passes along when you ask it to remember something.

  • Nothing is saved until you approve it

    Each memory is private unless you choose to share it with someone you’re connected to.

  • It never contacts anyone for you

    Heldly suggests. You send the message.
