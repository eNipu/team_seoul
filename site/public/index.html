You said you’d stay in touch.

Heldly is a ChatGPT plugin for keeping up with the people you care about. You tell it what matters, you approve what it saves, and you can ask it who you haven’t seen in a while.

How a good conversation goes cold

  1. That evening

    You meet someone and the conversation is good. You say “let’s stay in touch” and you mean it.

  2. The next day

    You swap contacts. LinkedIn, WhatsApp, maybe Instagram.

  3. A week later

    Life moves. You don’t know what they’re working on, and they don’t know what you need.

  4. A month later

    The connection has gone cold. You still care. Nothing reminded you.

  5. A year later

    You meet new people and hope this time it sticks.

Heldly works on the third step. It knows who you’re connected to, when you last logged time together, and which interests you both chose to share, so it can tell you who to get back to and suggest what to do.

Ask in the chat you already have open

Heldly has no app and no feed. You talk to it in ChatGPT and it answers there, sometimes with a card you can act on.

Remember that I’ve started bouldering.
Heldly shows a review card with what it would save. You untick, edit the wording, and choose who can see each memory. Nothing is saved until you approve it.
Create a Heldly invite code.
You get a code like K7QM-4XPD to send to a friend yourself. It lasts 72 hours. They redeem it in their own ChatGPT, you accept the request, and you’re connected.

A short list of connections you haven’t logged time with in three weeks or more, longest gap first.
What could Mina and I do this weekend?

What Heldly keeps, and what it doesn’t

Kept

  • Memories you approved, each with the visibility you chose. The default is private.
  • Your connections, and the dates you logged time together.
  • A display name, a short bio and your social goals, if you add them.

Not kept, not done

  • Heldly can’t read your ChatGPT conversations. It receives only the text ChatGPT passes to it when you ask it to remember something.
  • Suggestions you don’t approve are never added to your profile.
  • Private memories are never read on anyone else’s behalf, connected or not.
  • Heldly doesn’t message, email or invite anyone. You send the invite code yourself.
  • It refuses to store passwords, card numbers and API keys.

To draft suggestions, the server may send the text you asked it to remember to OpenAI’s API. You can delete a single memory at any time, or your whole account with everything attached to it.

Where it stands

Heldly is an early build. It runs in ChatGPT as a custom plugin and isn’t in the plugin directory yet, so for now you run the server yourself.

Get the code Read the setup guide

  1. Clone the repository, then run make setup and make dev.
  2. Put the server behind a public HTTPS address. A tunnel is enough for testing.
  3. In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Plugins in developer mode, and add a custom MCP server with your address ending in /mcp.
