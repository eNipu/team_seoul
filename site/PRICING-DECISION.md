# Venn pricing decision

Date: 22 November 2026. USD is the launch currency. EUR equivalents use an approximate 1 USD = EUR 0.91 conversion and should be rounded again before EU launch.

## Debate

### Growth lead

Charge the person who wants their network to work, not the people they invite. The invite loop is the product: if a friend hits a paywall, the loop breaks. Keep early access free and make the first paid plan feel like a small favor to the people you care about.

### CFO

Free-only is dangerous, but per-seat pricing is worse for this product. Start with a low base plan that pays for normal usage and a patron plan that lets high-intent users subsidize more free guests. Defer usage add-ons until we see real model and storage cost.

### Behavioral economist

"One subscription covers your circle" is easier to understand than credits, seats, or contact metering. A patron tier works because it gives people with higher willingness to pay a prosocial reason to choose it. Avoid pay-what-you-want at launch because it creates too much decision friction.

### Skeptical user

I already pay for ChatGPT, Spotify, storage, and five tiny apps. If Venn is another $20/month, I am out. If it is $5 and my friends never get charged because of me, I can try it without feeling weird.

## Moderator decision

Launch with three plans at most: Free Guest, Inner Circle, and Patron Circle. The product promise is simple: invited friends are always free, and paid members sponsor the circles they want to grow.

## Final plans

| Plan | Planned launch price | EUR equivalent | Who it is for | Included |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Free Guest | $0 forever | EUR 0 | Invited friends and light personal use | Accept invites, approve or stop sharing signals, answer question links, stay connected, and keep up to 10 private people. |
| Inner Circle | $5/month | ~EUR 4.50/month | The person who wants Venn to actively maintain their network | Up to 1,000 people, ChatGPT companion, reconnection nudges, private notes, question links, and 5 free friend passes. |
| Patron Circle | $10/month | ~EUR 9/month | People who can pay more and want to sponsor more of the network | Everything in Inner Circle, 10 free friend passes, priority early features, and a visible "supporting the circle" position only if the member opts in. |

Early access is free. The site should call these planned launch prices, not live billing.

## What is free forever

- Invited people never need to pay to accept, stay connected, approve what they share, edit it, stop sharing, or answer a link.
- A free guest can keep a small Venn of their own with up to 10 private people.
- Venn never charges someone because another person invited them.

## How "you + 5 friends" works

- An Inner Circle member gets 5 free friend passes.
- A pass gives that friend full Venn functionality inside the relationship with the paying member: they can share approved signals, receive context, edit or revoke sharing, and stay connected for free.
- The paying member can have more lightweight contacts and question-link replies, but 5 active shared friend passes are included at launch.
- A friend who upgrades keeps every existing connection and receives their own 5 passes. This is how the network expands without forcing invited people to pay.
- Patron Circle includes 10 passes for people who want to sponsor more free participation.

## Founding-member offer for the waitlist

Founding Circle members get early access free. When billing starts, they can keep Inner Circle at $5/month for as long as they stay subscribed, or choose $50/year for the first year. Founders also get 10 passes for the first 12 months so the initial network can form faster.

## What we test first

- Waitlist conversion from "early access is free" versus "planned $5/month at launch."
- Whether "5 free friend passes" is clearer than "you + 5 friends."
- Whether Patron Circle attracts supporters without cannibalizing Inner Circle.
- Whether 10-contact free personal use is enough for invited friends to form a habit.
- Whether users fill passes before they ask for extra capacity.
